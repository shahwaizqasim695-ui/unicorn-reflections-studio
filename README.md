# Rich Higgins Poetry

Create a complete, modern, premium poetry author website for Rich Higgins at the domain RichHigginsPoetry.com.
Brand & Feel

Elegant, literary, and high-end
Soft, atmospheric, slightly mystical mood that matches the book cover (white unicorn rising from dark ocean waves under dramatic clouds)
Color palette inspired by the book cover: deep navy/midnight blue, soft gold accents, pearl white, muted sky blues and warm cream
Premium typography (serif for headings, clean elegant sans for body)
Smooth animations, soft fades, and refined hover effects
Mobile-first, fully responsive, fast-loading

Pages Required

Home
About
The Book (description + cover image + purchase CTA)
Order Form (clean form to order the book)
Blog
Forum
FAQ
Contact (footer + dedicated contact section)

Content Guidelines

Author: Rich Higgins
Book Title: Unicorns and Other Reflections
Publisher: The Collingwood Press
Use the provided book cover image as the hero visual and main product image
Include a short elegant author bio section
Highlight that the poems explore nature, memory, coaching, magic, and reflection
Feature a short testimonial quote from Dr. Kathleen P. Decker (Past President, The Poetry Society of Virginia)
Keep language refined and literary

Design Requirements

Sticky elegant navigation with: About | The Book | Order | Blog | Forum | FAQ
Footer with: Contact, About, Blog, and copyright
Hero section on homepage with full-bleed book cover artwork + soft overlay text
High-quality book mockup display on “The Book” page
Clean, spacious layout with generous white space
Subtle paper/texture or soft gradient backgrounds where appropriate
Professional order form (name, email, address, quantity, message)
Soft gold buttons with refined hover states

Technical Notes

Modern clean HTML/CSS/JS or React/Next.js preferred
Semantic structure and good accessibility
SEO-friendly (proper titles, meta, headings)
Easy to maintain and expand later

Please generate the full website structure, beautiful UI, and sample content for all pages. Make it feel like a premium independent poetry press site.
